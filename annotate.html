🐦 Bird Call Annotator

Draw rectangles on the spectrogram to mark where the bird's call appears. Mark EACH occurrence of the call (including repeats).
How to use:
1. Select a bird from the dropdown
2. Click and drag on the spectrogram to draw a rectangle around ONE call
3. Repeat for each time the call appears (shows timing pattern)
4. Click "Save Annotations" to save to the JSON file
5. Move to the next bird

Tips: Draw tight rectangles. Include the full frequency range of the call. The x-axis is time, y-axis is frequency (low at bottom, high at top).
