Section 1: How to prepare a bird recording

Using Chirp Coach Bird Recording Studio

Afdeling 1: Hoe om 'n voëlopname voor te berei

Met Chirp Coach Voëlopname-ateljee
1

Get close to the bird

The single most important factor in a quality recording is distance. Move as close to the bird as possible without disturbing it. A recording made 5 m away will always be better than one made 20 m away.

  • Move slowly and quietly
  • Avoid rustling clothing or footsteps
  • Point your phone or microphone directly at the bird

Kom nader aan die voël

Die belangrikste faktor vir 'n goeie opname is afstand. Beweeg so naby as moontlik aan die voël sonder om dit te steur. 'n Opname van 5 m weg sal altyd beter wees as een van 20 m weg.

  • Beweeg stadig en stil
  • Vermy ruisende klere of stapgeluide
  • Rig jou foon of mikrofoon direk op die voël
TipEarly morning (within 1–2 hours after sunrise) is best — birds are most active and background noise is lowest.
WenkVroeë oggend (binne 1–2 uur na sonopkoms) is die beste — voëls is die aktiefste en agtergrondgeraas is die laagste.
2

Open the Bird Recording Studio

From the Chirp Coach home screen, tap Bird Recording Studio. Allow microphone access when prompted.

Maak die Voëlopname-ateljee oop

Vanaf die Chirp Coach-tuisskerm, tik op Voëlopname-ateljee. Laat mikrofoon-toegang toe wanneer gevra word.

3

Watch the level meter

When pressing REC, watch the level meter while the bird is calling. The bar should reach roughly the middle to two-thirds of the meter. The small vertical marker shows the recent peak.

  • Too low (bar barely moves) — move closer
  • Red / clipping — move further away or lower the phone
  • Aim for a GOOD SIGNAL ✓ status

Kyk na die vlak-meter

Voordat jy REC druk, kyk na die vlak-meter terwyl die voël roep. Die balk moet ongeveer die middel tot twee-derdes van die meter bereik. Die klein vertikale merker wys die onlangse piek.

  • Te laag (balk beweeg skaars) — beweeg nader
  • Rooi / knip — beweeg verder weg of laer die foon
  • Mik na 'n GOEIE SEIN ✓ status
Screenshot: Level meter with GOOD SIGNAL status
4

Press REC and record

Tap the red ● REC button. Record for at least 15–30 seconds to capture several calls. The live spectrogram will show the bird's calls as dark bands — this is a good sign.

Druk REC en neem op

Tik die rooi ● REC knoppie. Neem minstens 15–30 sekondes op om verskeie roepgeluide vas te vang. Die lewendige spektrogram wys die voël se roepgeluide as donker bande — dit is 'n goeie teken.

Screenshot: Active recording showing live spectrogram with bird calls
TipHold your phone steady and breathe slowly. Even small movements can cause noise. Do not cover the microphone with your fingers.
WenkHou jou foon stil en asem stadig. Selfs klein bewegings kan geraas veroorsaak. Moenie die mikrofoon met jou vingers toemaak nie.
5

Stop and review

Tap ⏹ Stop. The waveform and full spectrogram will be displayed. Look for clear, distinct call patterns in the spectrogram — these are the parts worth keeping.

Stop en hersien

Tik ⏹ Stop. Die golfvorm en volle spektrogram word vertoon. Soek na duidelike, onderskeibare roeppatrone in die spektrogram — dit is die dele werd om te hou.

Screenshot: Post-recording view with waveform and spectrogram
6

Edit out unwanted sections (optional)

Drag on the waveform to select a region. Then choose:

  • ✂ Remove selection — cuts that section out (good for removing noise in the middle)
  • Keep selection only — discards everything outside (good for isolating the best call)

You can make multiple edits. The original is always preserved — tap ↩ Reset to original to undo everything.

Sny ongewenste dele uit (opsioneel)

Sleep op die golfvorm om 'n streek te kies. Kies dan:

  • ✂ Verwyder seleksie — sny daardie deel uit (goed om geraas in die middel te verwyder)
  • Hou slegs seleksie — gooi alles buite die seleksie weg (goed om die beste roepgeluid uit te sonder)

Jy kan verskeie redigerings maak. Die oorspronklike word altyd bewaar — tik ↩ Terug na oorspronklike om alles ongedaan te maak.

Screenshot: Waveform with selection region highlighted
7

Add metadata and save

Fill in Species, Location, Date and Time while you still remember them — this becomes part of the filename. Then tap ⬇ Save WAV. The file is saved directly to your device.

Voeg metadata by en stoor

Vul Spesie, Ligging, Datum en Tyd in terwyl jy dit nog onthou — dit word deel van die lêernaam. Tik dan ⬇ Stoor WAV. Die lêer word direk op jou toestel gestoor.

Screenshot: Metadata fields filled in with save button
File namingFiles are named automatically, e.g. Berg_and_Dal_Black-headed_oriole_20260816_1518.wav
LêernamingLêers word outomaties vernoem, bv. Berg_and_Dal_Black-headed_oriole_20260816_1518.wav

Section 2: Opening a recording from another app

Edit recordings made with Merlin, Voice Recorder, or any other app

Afdeling 2: Maak 'n opname van 'n ander program oop

Redigeer opnames gemaak met Merlin, Voice Recorder, of enige ander program
Good to knowChirp Coach Studio can open and edit audio files from any app — Merlin Bird ID, your phone's built-in Voice Recorder, WhatsApp audio, etc. The original file on your device is never changed.
Goed om te weetChirp Coach-ateljee kan oudio-lêers van enige program oopmaak en redigeer — Merlin Bird ID, jou foon se ingeboude opnemer, WhatsApp-oudio, ens. Die oorspronklike lêer op jou toestel word nooit verander nie.
1

Save your recording to your device's files

In the app you recorded with, find the recording and export it to your device's file storage. Most apps have a Share or Export button.

  • Merlin Bird ID — open the recording, tap the share icon ↗, choose "Save to Files"
  • Voice Recorder (Samsung/Android) — tap ⋮ on the recording, choose "Share" or "Export"
  • iPhone Voice Memos — tap the recording, tap "…", choose "Save to Files"

Stoor jou opname na jou toestel se lêers

In die program wat jy gebruik het, vind die opname en voer dit uit na jou toestel se lêerstoor. Die meeste programme het 'n Deel of Uitvoer-knoppie.

  • Merlin Bird ID — maak die opname oop, tik die deel-ikoon ↗, kies "Stoor na lêers"
  • Voice Recorder (Samsung/Android) — tik ⋮ op die opname, kies "Deel" of "Uitvoer"
  • iPhone Voice Memos — tik die opname, tik "…", kies "Stoor na lêers"
Screenshot: Merlin app share / export recording screen
2

Tap "📂 Open existing recording" in Chirp Coach Studio

Open the Bird Recording Studio. At the top of the screen, tap the 📂 Open existing recording button — this is separate from the REC button and lets you load any saved audio file.

Tik "📂 Open bestaande opname" in Chirp Coach-ateljee

Maak die Voëlopname-ateljee oop. Bo-aan die skerm, tik die 📂 Open bestaande opname-knoppie — dit is apart van die REC-knoppie en laat jou enige gestoorde oudio-lêer laai.

Screenshot: Studio screen with "Open existing recording" button highlighted
3

Select your audio file

Your device's file picker opens. Navigate to where you saved the recording (e.g. Downloads, or the Merlin folder) and tap the file to load it. Supported formats: WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, M4A, AAC.

Kies jou oudio-lêer

Jou toestel se lêerkieser open. Navigeer na waar jy die opname gestoor het (bv. Aflaaifolder, of die Merlin-folder) en tik die lêer om dit te laai. Ondersteunde formate: WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, M4A, AAC.

Screenshot: Device file picker showing audio files
4

The waveform and spectrogram load automatically

The waveform and spectrogram are displayed — just like after a live recording. A file info bar at the top shows the format, duration, and sample rate. All editing tools are now available.

Die golfvorm en spektrogram laai outomaties

Die golfvorm en spektrogram word vertoon — net soos na 'n lewendige opname. 'n Lêer-inligtingsbalk bo-aan wys die formaat, duur en samplekoers. Al die redigeernutsgoed is nou beskikbaar.

Screenshot: Waveform and spectrogram loaded from external file
5

Edit and save

Drag on the waveform to select a region, then use the editing tools:

  • Keep selection only — keep only the selected part (e.g. isolate the best call)
  • ✂ Remove selection — cut out the selected part (e.g. remove noise)
  • ⊣ Trim start / Trim end ⊢ — trim to the edges of your selection
  • ↩ Undo / ↪ Redo — step back or forward through your edits

When done, fill in the metadata and tap ⬇ Save edited WAV. The original file is never modified — only the new edited copy is saved.

Redigeer en stoor

Sleep op die golfvorm om 'n streek te kies, gebruik dan die redigeernutsgoed:

  • Hou slegs seleksie — hou net die gekose deel (bv. die beste roep uitsonder)
  • ✂ Verwyder seleksie — sny die gekose deel uit (bv. geraas verwyder)
  • ⊣ Sny begin / Sny einde ⊢ — sny tot by die rande van jou seleksie
  • ↩ Ongedaan / ↪ Herdoen — stap terug of vorentoe deur jou redigerings

Vul die metadata in en tik ⬇ Stoor geredigeerde WAV. Die oorspronklike lêer word nooit gewysig nie — slegs die nuwe geredigeerde kopie word gestoor.

Screenshot: Editing tools with selection and save buttons

Section 3: How to upload to eBird / Macaulay Library

Share your recording with the world's largest bird sound archive

Afdeling 3: Hoe om op te laai na eBird / Macaulay-biblioteek

Deel jou opname met die wêreld se grootste voëlklankargiefwerk
The Macaulay Library only accepts recordings where the species is confirmed. Make sure you are certain of the identification before uploading.
Die Macaulay-biblioteek aanvaar slegs opnames waar die spesie bevestig is. Maak seker jy is seker van die identifikasie voordat jy oplaai.
1

Create a free eBird account

Go to ebird.org and create a free account if you don't have one. The same account gives you access to the Macaulay Library.

Skep 'n gratis eBird-rekening

Gaan na ebird.org en skep 'n gratis rekening as jy nie een het nie. Dieselfde rekening gee jou toegang tot die Macaulay-biblioteek.

Screenshot: eBird.org sign-up / login page
2

Tap Submit and choose your location

In the eBird app, tap Submit at the bottom. You will be asked "Where did you bird?" — choose Find it on a map.

Tik Submit en kies jou ligging

In die eBird-program, tik Submit onderaan. Jy sal gevra word "Where did you bird?" — kies Find it on a map.

3

Search for your location on the map

A map opens. You can either zoom in to the exact location where you birded, or type the name of the hotspot in the search bar — e.g. Berg-en-Dal Rest Camp. Tap OK when you have positioned the map correctly.

Soek jou ligging op die kaart

A kaart open. Jy kan inzoem na die presiese ligging waar jy gebird het, of die naam van die hotspot in die soekbalk intik — bv. Berg-en-Dal Rest Camp. Tik OK wanneer jy die kaart reg geposisioneer het.

Screenshot: eBird map screen with hotspot search bar
4

Select the hotspot and tap Continue

Tap the hotspot pin to activate it — the chosen location will be shown, e.g. Kruger NP–Berg-en-Dal Camp. Then tap Continue to proceed.

Kies die hotspot en tik Continue

Tik die hotspot-pen om dit te aktiveer — die gekose ligging sal gewys word, bv. Kruger NP–Berg-en-Dal Camp. Tik dan Continue om voort te gaan.

Screenshot: eBird map with hotspot pin selected and Continue button
5

Choose Observation Date and Type

Set the date of your observation, then choose the observation type that best describes how you birded:

  • Traveling — you moved more than 30 m during the session (enter approximate distance)
  • Stationary — you stayed within a 30 m area (enter duration)
  • Historical — birding was your primary purpose, but you don't know the exact start time, duration or distance
  • Incidental — birding was not your primary purpose; you noticed some birds while doing something else

If you know the start time and/or duration, fill those in even for Historical.

Kies Waarnemingsdatum en -tipe

Stel die datum van jou waarneming, en kies dan die waarnemingstipe wat die beste beskryf hoe jy gebird het:

  • Traveling — jy het meer as 30 m beweeg gedurende die sessie (sit die afstand in)
  • Stationary — jy het binne 'n 30 m area gebly (sit die duur in)
  • Historical — voëlkyk was jou primêre doel, maar jy weet nie die presiese begintyd, duur of afstand nie
  • Incidental — voëlkyk was nie jou primêre doel nie; jy het toevallig voëls opgemerk

As jy die begintyd en/of duur ken, vul dit in selfs vir Historical.

Screenshot: eBird Observation Date and Type selection screen
6

Add the species you saw or heard

Search for and add each species to your list. For each one, enter the number of individuals you counted. If you are unsure of the exact count, enter X (meaning present but not counted).

TipTry to be as accurate as possible, but X is always better than no record at all.

Voeg die spesies by wat jy gesien of gehoor het

Soek en voeg elke spesie by jou lys. Vir elkeen, sit die aantal individue in wat jy getel het. As jy nie seker is van die presiese getal nie, sit 'n X in (beteken teenwoordig maar nie getel nie).

WenkProbeer so akkuraat as moontlik wees, maar X is altyd beter as geen rekord glad nie.
Screenshot: eBird species entry screen with counts and X entries
7

Answer the Complete Checklist question

eBird will ask: "Are you reporting all species detected?"

Answer Yes if you recorded every species you were able to identify to the best of your ability — not just highlights. You do not need to count every individual (you can use X), but you should try to report all species.

Answer No if you are deliberately leaving some species out (e.g. you only recorded the one bird and ignored all others).

If you used Incidental as your observation type, the complete checklist question does not apply.

Beantwoord die Volledige voëllys-vraag

eBird sal vra: "Are you reporting all species detected?"

Antwoord Yes indien jy elke spesie aangemeld het wat jy kon identifiseer — nie net hoogtepunte nie. Jy hoef nie elke individu te tel nie (jy kan X gebruik), maar probeer alle spesies rapporteer.

Antwoord No indien jy doelbewus sommige spesies uitlaat (bv. jy het net die een voël opgeneem en alle ander geïgnoreer).

As jy Incidental as tipe gebruik het, is hierdie vraag nie van toepassing nie.
Screenshot: eBird complete checklist Yes/No screen
8

Submit your checklist, then tap "Add/Manage Media"

Tap Submit to submit your checklist. After submitting, a confirmation screen appears. Tap Add/Manage Media — this is where you attach your bird recording to a specific species on the checklist.

Dien jou voëllys in, tik dan "Add/Manage Media"

Tik Submit om jou voëllys in te dien. Na indiening verskyn 'n bevestigingskerm. Tik Add/Manage Media — hier koppel jy jou voëlopname aan 'n spesifieke spesie op die voëllys.

Replace with: Screenshot: eBird submission confirmation with Manage Media button
9

Attach your recording to the species

Find the species you recorded in the Manage Media screen. Tap the media icon (camera/audio icon) next to it, select Add media, and choose your WAV or MP3 file from your device's storage.

Koppel jou opname aan die spesie

Vind die spesie wat jy opgeneem het in die Manage Media-skerm. Tik die media-ikoon (kamera/klank-ikoon) langs dit, kies Add media, en kies jou WAV- of MP3-lêer vanuit jou toestel se stoor.

Replace with: Screenshot: eBird Manage Media screen with media attachment button
10

Add recording details for the Macaulay Library

After uploading, fill in the details required by the Macaulay Library:

  • Species — confirm it is correct
  • Behaviours — e.g. Song, Call, Alarm call
  • Recording equipment — e.g. Smartphone, built-in microphone
  • Background sounds — note any other species audible in the recording

Voeg opname-besonderhede by vir die Macaulay-biblioteek

Na die oplaai, vul die besonderhede in wat die Macaulay-biblioteek benodig:

  • Spesie — bevestig dit is korrek
  • Gedrag — bv. Sang, Roep, Alarmroep
  • Opname-toerusting — bv. Slimfoon, ingeboude mikrofoon
  • Agtergrondgeluide — let op enige ander spesies hoorbaar in die opname
Replace with: Screenshot: Macaulay Library media details entry form
11

Your recording is submitted!

Your recording will be reviewed and added to the Macaulay Library — the world's largest archive of wildlife audio and video, managed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Jou opname is ingedien!

Jou opname sal hersien word en by die Macaulay-biblioteek gevoeg word — die wêreld se grootste argief van wildlewe-oudio en -video, bestuur deur die Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Quality countsEven smartphone recordings are valuable — thousands of species have very few recordings. Your contribution matters!
Gehalte telSelfs slimfoon-opnames is waardevol — duisende spesies het baie min opnames. Jou bydrae maak saak!
