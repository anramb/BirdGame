Southern Africa bird occurrence and seasonal reporting-rate data in this app is derived from the Southern African Bird Atlas Project 2 (SABAP2) / African Bird Atlas Project, a citizen-science project coordinated by the FitzPatrick Institute of African Ornithology, University of Cape Town, in partnership with BirdLife South Africa and SANBI.
